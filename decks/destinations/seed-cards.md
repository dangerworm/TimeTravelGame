# Destination Deck — Seed Cards

_A destination card is an **era card**: a left-to-right **path of steps**, each printing a single
**skill requirement** (Insight / Craft / Grit) you meet by playing matching chips from your hand.
Most steps are pure **gates** (no reward); the **second-to-last** step holds a **find** (Cash); the
**last** step is the **objective**. **Danger** steps are open **Grit** checks (anyone brave can
answer); **knowledge** steps are **profession-locked** (only the right mind unlocks them). The
objective is claimed by **Recording** it (evidence → Reputation, history intact) or **Plundering**
it (take it → Sell for Cash or Publish for Reputation later) — and plundering a **non-doomed**
artefact **scars the timeline**, while a **doomed** one (about to burn / be lost) grabs clean._

_These five are Drew's originals (mined from `prototype-01`, 5 Jun 2026), re-cast into the v4 model
as the **tonal + structural benchmark** for the deck. Requirements, eras and step-counts are
illustrative seeds — calibrate against the sim's recommended curve when building the real deck.
Between them they show every prize type: a scarring plunder, a record-only cipher, and three doomed
grabs that reward reading the fiction._

---

## The Great Stink — London, 1858

_Era: **Modern**_

> Bazalgette's prototype relief valve was cast at the Deptford works weeks before the official design
> was filed. Recover it before the record catches up — and decide whether the world should ever know
> it existed.

**The path (→):**

1. Decipher the unpublished schematics — **Insight 2** _(Historian-locked)_
2. Slip into the half-built pumping works — **Grit 2** _(danger)_ · _find: Cash_
3. Retrieve the prototype valve — **Craft 3** · _objective_

**Prize:** the valve is a real, surviving artefact. **Record** it (measure and document, leave it in
the record) for clean Reputation; or **Plunder** it for Cash — but removing it **scars the timeline**
(the cast history now diverges). Not doomed: taking it is greedy, not clever.

## The Astronomer's Margins — Nuremberg, 1471

_Era: **Medieval**_

> Regiomontanus kept a second set of notes he never published — calculations decades ahead of their
> time. A footprint in the wrong cathedral courtyard here would be unlike anything the night watch
> has ever seen.

**The path (→):**

1. Read the marginal cipher — **Insight 2** _(Historian-locked)_
2. Cross the cathedral works unseen — **Grit 2** _(danger)_
3. Win the confidence of the workshop apprentice — **Insight 2** · _find: Cash_
4. Copy the hidden notes — **Insight 3** · _objective_

**Prize:** pure knowledge — there is no object to take, only marginalia to transcribe. **Record-only**
(copy and publish). The cleanest kind of legacy: you were never there.

## Priestley's Lost Apparatus — Birmingham, 1791

_Era: **Early Modern**_

> Days before the Birmingham rioters burn his laboratory, Priestley seals an experimental apparatus
> in the cellar. Reaching it means slipping past a gathering mob — and the building won't survive the
> night.

**The path (→):**

1. Locate the cellar from the half-burned records — **Insight 2** _(Historian-locked)_
2. Divert the rioters from the east door — **Grit 3** _(danger)_
3. Work the cellar lock before the smoke thickens — **Craft 2** · _find: Cash_
4. Lift the sealed apparatus clear — **Craft 2** · _objective_

**Prize:** **doomed** — the lab burns to the ground hours later, apparatus and all. **Plundering it is
clean _and_ clever** (you saved what history was about to lose): take it for Cash or Publish it for
Reputation, **no timeline scar**. The player who read the brief is rewarded.

## Before the Fire — Alexandria, 48 BC

_Era: **Ancient**_

> A scroll catalogued exactly once, the week before the great library burned. This is deep time — the
> record is gone, so you cannot know everything you'll meet down there.

**The path (→):**

1. Talk your way past the librarians — **Insight 3** _(Historian-locked)_
2. A scholar realises you don't belong — slip the question — **Grit 2** _(danger)_
3. Navigate the unlit lower stacks — **Insight 2**
4. Gather the loose ostraca as cover — **Craft 2** · _find: Cash_
5. Seize the doomed scroll — **Insight 3** · _objective_

**Prize:** **doomed** — the library is ash within days. Why copy what's about to burn? **Grab it** —
clean Cash or a career-making paper, **no scar**. The signature "read the fiction" destination.

## The Engineer's Warning — Lisbon, 1755

_Era: **Early Modern**_

> Moments before the great earthquake, an engineer's seismic notes still lie on his desk. Grab them
> and get out as the ground opens and the city comes down around you.

**The path (→):**

1. Find the notes in the failing light — **Insight 2**
2. Brace the collapsing stair — **Craft 3** _(danger)_
3. Cross the buckling floor to the desk — **Grit 2** · _find: Cash_
4. Escape the tremor with the notes — **Grit 2** · _objective_

**Prize:** **doomed** — desk, study and street are gone minutes later. **Take the notes clean** (Cash
or Publish); leaving them to "preserve the record" preserves nothing but rubble. A deep, volatile,
high-reward jump where the danger is the setting itself.
