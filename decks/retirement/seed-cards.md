# Retirement Deck — Seed Cards

_The Retirement deck is drawn from **only** when a researcher is retired **at or above the
experience threshold** (i.e. one you actually invested in and grew). Retiring a below-threshold
junior just frees the slot — they move on, no draw._

_Each card is an **upgrade**, a **boon**, a **reputation bonus**, or a **protégé**, with flavour
text describing how the legacy came about. Some are pure gifts; some are **opportunities that carry
a cost** (you choose whether to take them). The tone: warm, character-driven, occasionally dark or
funny — the game's voice._

_These four are Drew's originals (5 Jun 2026), kept as the tonal benchmark for the deck._

---

## The Blueprint (machine upgrade)

> You hold a goodbye party for the researcher and they get emotional, revealing that they're scared
> of retiring because they don't know how they'll fill the time. You and other close colleagues on
> the team promise to keep in touch.
>
> That closeness pays off. The retiring researcher privately resolves to solve one of the stickiest
> problems with the time machine, and a couple of years later at a coffee meet-up they present you
> with a blueprint for an upgrade to the machine — at no cost.

**Effect:** Gain one machine upgrade for free.

## The Honey Trap (opportunity, with a cost)

> The typically-quiet researcher leaves on their last day with no more than an "it's been great, I
> wish you the best of luck" and toddles off. Your curiosity about their coldness makes you wonder
> what they're hiding, and you get an engineer to check their email outbox. It turns out they were
> being blackmailed by a competing organisation and had been feeding intelligence on your operation
> to other teams. You decide to spring a honey trap and feed the channel (which they conveniently
> left open) with misinformation, leading to that organisation's demise. The sudden uptick in
> unemployment means you get your pick of researchers!

**Effect:** Recruit any one available researcher, at −4 Cash and −3 reputation. _(Open: should the
−3 be **disrepute tokens** rather than a rep loss? The honey-trap deception is an ethics-linked act —
Drew to rule.)_

## The Circuit (reputation bonus)

> The researcher has a wonderful send-off and soon begins giving talks at universities around the
> world. They often get questions about their time on your team, and your reputation soars.

**Effect:** Gain [X] reputation.

## The Protégé (lineage)

> The researcher seems a little overwhelmed on their last day and comes to you. They tell you
> they've been moonlighting as a professor at a local university and have met a wonderful
> postdoctoral researcher who they think would fit right into your organisation.

**Effect:** Recruit a postdoc matching this researcher's field, at no cost.
