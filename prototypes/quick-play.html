WARPED — Quick Play

A first taste of the core expedition loop. 3 rival scientists, ~20 minutes.

GOAL. Most Reputation after everyone has taken 3 turns wins.

THE DICE. Roll an ordinary d6 and read its pips from the table. Each expedition says which die it uses.

d6 → 1 2 3 4 5 6
Die A (steady) 0 1 1 1 1 2
Die B (wild) 0 0 1 1 2 2

SETUP. Shared Timeline Integrity = 10 (track it on paper). Shuffle the expedition deck; lay 3 face-up = the Offer. Every player uses the same Team (below). Grab 2–3 ordinary d6 and something to score on.

ON YOUR TURN

  1. Choose one expedition from the 3-card Offer.
  2. Run it left → right. For each step:
    1. Assign one researcher whose specialism matches.
    2. Success: Skill ≥ Need → automatic. Short by exactly 1 → you may OVERCLOCK: this step’s Danger +1, then roll (Skill+1) dice — pips ≥ Need = success. Short by 2+ or no match → can’t; the expedition ends here.
    3. Fatigue: a researcher working an extra step this expedition takes −1 Discretion on it. (Okafor leads: once per expedition, ignore one such −1.)
    4. Footprint (if Danger ≥ 1, win or lose): roll Danger-many dice, sum pips, − the assigned Discretion = imprint (min 0, capped by the era). Drop Timeline Integrity by that much.
    5. Bank the step’s reward.
  3. Cash out? Before starting any step you may stop and keep everything banked — a safe exit.
  4. Objective (★ last step) holds the big prize. Some offer PUBLISH (fewer Rep, leaves history clean) or PLUNDER (more Rep now, but +1 imprint to the shared timeline). DOOMED prizes are about to be destroyed anyway — so plundering them is free (no extra imprint). Read the fiction!
  5. Fail a gate → expedition ends; keep what you banked. Refill the Offer to 3.

THE TIMELINE BITES (shared greed). While Integrity is ≤ 6, every step gets +1 Danger. While ≤ 3, +2 Danger. Everyone’s greed makes everyone’s jumps riskier.

Tags: TWIST flip on reveal (Need changes) · HIDDEN revealed only when you reach it · THREAD an intervention you must close at the objective, or it snaps (Integrity −2, no objective reward) · DOOMED the prize is lost to history regardless.

END. After each player’s 3rd turn, highest Reputation wins. If Integrity ever hits 0 the timeline is collapsing — finish the round and crown the most reputable (we’ll design the real collapse later).
